How to use this guide

Choose one of the 12 tenses. First study its structure, then read the examples and Sinhala meanings. Select an example's Show Passive button to see the matching passive sentence with an animation.

01 · StructureLearn the correct word order and helping verbs.
02 · Sentence formsCompare affirmative, negative, question and negative-question patterns.
03 · Voice & practiceExplore active/passive voice and test your understanding.
Grammar note: Passive voice is not natural or commonly used with every tense. The guide shows standard passive patterns where they are grammatically possible and marks rare forms clearly.
